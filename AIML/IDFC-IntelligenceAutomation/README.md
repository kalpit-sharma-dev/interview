## How to use this pack (fastest path)

1. Read **00-End-to-End-Plan.md** fully — it tells you what to prioritize given your background.
2. Skim **01-End-to-End-Guide.md** to internalize the *narrative* (how to talk about the role).
3. Use **02-Study-Material.md** to close knowledge gaps (decisioning engines, event-driven, governance).
4. Drill **03-Study-Guide.md** flashcards + diagrams daily.
5. Rehearse out loud from **04-500-Interview-Questions.md** — pick 20–30/day; record yourself.
